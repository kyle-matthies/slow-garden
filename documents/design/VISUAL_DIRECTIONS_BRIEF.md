# Visual directions brief

Status: Three directions generated; awaiting selection and evaluation
Date: 2026-08-25
Target: Responsive desktop web first, 1440 x 1024 concept frame

## Experience to express

Slow Garden is a quiet thinking surface, not a dashboard. The primary screen must make it effortless to add and spatially arrange unfinished source notes while clearly reserving AI interpretation for a later, bounded return. The visual system should feel alive without demanding attention.

The official Flower reference is a moodboard for natural serenity, open space, movement, accumulated petals, and an environment that changes through gentle interaction. The product must not reproduce game assets, scenes, or trade dress.

## Hard constraints

- Source notes dominate the working state and remain visually distinct from derived blooms.
- At most three blooms appear in a return.
- Provenance, uncertainty, and correction are reachable without turning the garden into a report.
- Minimal persistent chrome; no chatbot transcript, analytics dashboard, kanban board, or dense left navigation.
- Text remains readable over all atmospheric art.
- Motion communicates gathering, processing, reveal, or correction—not idle decoration.
- Every meaningful animation has a reduced-motion state.
- The concept must translate to iPhone review and capture even if spatial arrangement simplifies.

## Direction hypotheses

### Windborne Meadow

An open pale-cyan sky and bright, shallow-focus meadow create a spacious canvas. Source seeds sit like light paper petals near the ground plane. Fine wind traces gently relate material. A return gathers a few loose petals into one luminous bloom without covering the writing surface. This is the closest emotional translation of Flower and the strongest test of radical calm.

Risk: atmosphere may overpower legibility or make serious work feel whimsical.

### Living Constellation

A deep dusk-to-cyan field treats seeds as quiet islands of text and blooms as luminous botanical constellations. Thin paths and drifting particles make relationships visible across distance. The reveal can form text from light without literal flowers dominating the interface.

Risk: familiar “AI graph” aesthetics could make it feel technical, magical, or falsely intelligent.

### Pressed Botanical Studio

A warm off-white botanical notebook uses restrained specimen color, generous typography, and tactile evidence layers. Seeds resemble editable field notes; blooms resemble pressed specimens with source tabs and dated lineage. Growth is expressed through layout and subtle pigment rather than continuous motion.

Risk: the safest and most evidence-forward direction may lose the feeling of transformation.

## Required frames within each direction

Each generated direction should concentrate on one primary Garden screen and imply, rather than inventory, the full product. It should visibly include:

- several short user-authored seed notes arranged spatially;
- an unintrusive way to add a seed;
- calm pass/cadence status;
- one newly returned bloom with a clear derived-state label;
- one visible provenance cue that can open exact sources;
- a restrained route to keep, correct, or prune;
- realistic, readable product copy rather than placeholder text.

Return Reveal and Bloom Detail will be designed after a direction is selected; the primary frame must prove the visual grammar can support both.

## Selection rubric

Score each direction from 1 to 5 and record evidence, not preference alone.

| Criterion | Weight | Test |
|---|---:|---|
| Quiet capture | 20% | Can a person write without animation or system output competing for attention? |
| Source versus derived clarity | 20% | Can viewers correctly identify what Kyle wrote and what the system inferred? |
| Provenance and correction | 15% | Is evidence inspection discoverable without permanent detail density? |
| Accumulation and delayed return | 15% | Does the screen make waiting and later transformation legible? |
| Flower-inspired emotional quality | 10% | Does it convey serenity, movement, and environmental change without imitation? |
| Readability and scale | 10% | Can the language survive 5, 25, and eventually 100+ seeds? |
| Accessibility and reduced motion | 5% | Does meaning survive without color and movement? |
| Mobile translation | 5% | Can capture and bloom review remain coherent on iPhone? |

Automatic rejection conditions: source and AI content are confused; text is illegible; the visual relies on constant motion; provenance is hidden; the concept looks primarily like chat, a dashboard, or a generic node graph.
