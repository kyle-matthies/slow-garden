## Findings

### Windborne Meadow

Strongest emotional expression of open air, gentle accumulation, and a transformed environment. Capture feels spacious and the bloom is scarce. The full-bleed landscape competes with long-term readability, and free-floating petal cards could become unstable at higher density. The visual also risks making product and strategy work feel more whimsical than serious.

Keep if selected: cyan openness, light wind paths, scarce coral petals, and the bloom gathering at the edge rather than taking over.

Correct before build: establish a calmer neutral reading plane, reduce photographic dependence, and prove a 25-seed state plus reduced motion.

### Living Constellation

Best expression of distant relationships and material gathering across space. The dark field supports focused return review. It is the closest to a familiar AI knowledge graph, the seed circles feel categorical, and fine gold text/lines may fail contrast or low-power conditions. The luminous bloom can imply intelligence or certainty beyond the evidence.

Keep if selected: restrained dusk palette, long-distance path grammar, and focus around one returned connection.

Correct before build: replace circles with more editable note forms, reduce magical glow, strengthen source labels, and provide a light-mode equivalent.

### Pressed Botanical Studio

Strongest source-versus-derived grammar, evidence visibility, correction affordance, typography, and reduced-motion path. It can support serious work and provenance without relying on animation. The current frame is denser and more editorial than spatial, and it risks becoming a botanical report board rather than a living thinking garden.

Keep if selected: source slips, specimen-versus-interpretation distinction, evidence tabs, paper warmth, and precise correction controls.

Correct before build: restore more open spatial composition, hide evidence detail until requested, reduce literal dried-flower decoration, and borrow a small amount of Windborne’s environmental transformation.

## Preliminary recommendation

Pressed Botanical Studio is the safest H1 foundation because trust and evidence are harder to retrofit than atmosphere. Windborne Meadow provides the stronger emotional north star. A disciplined hybrid could use Pressed Botanical’s semantic grammar inside Windborne’s open, changing environment, but no hybrid should be generated until Kyle chooses a primary direction or explicitly asks for refinement.

Living Constellation should be preserved as a rejected alternative unless Kyle strongly prefers it; it carries the greatest risk of generic AI-graph and magical-intelligence cues.

## Selection and test gate

After Kyle selects or refines a direction:

1. produce Garden, Return Reveal, and Bloom Detail at matching fidelity;
2. create 5-, 25-, and 100-seed states;
3. create reduced-motion, keyboard-focus, large-text, and iPhone review frames;
4. run a five-scenario comprehension test for source, inference, research, contradiction, and correction;
5. record the selected direction and preserved rejected alternatives in the decision log.

The direction is accepted only if all participants correctly distinguish source from system material in the core scenarios and provenance is found without instruction by at least 80% of tests.
