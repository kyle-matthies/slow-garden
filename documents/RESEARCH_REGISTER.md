## Baselines to preserve

- A normal journal with no AI.
- A journal with immediate per-entry AI reflection.
- A conventional weekly summary with themes and mood trends.
- A canvas or notes app with manual spatial organization.
- A chat-based general AI asked to synthesize exported entries.

## Core evaluation dimensions

- Source faithfulness.
- Useful surprise.
- Novel connection versus obvious summary.
- Emotional and interpretive overreach.
- Preservation of user voice.
- Traceability and correction quality.
- Cognitive load and return density.
- Value attributable to accumulation or delay.
- Privacy comfort and trust.
- Cost and processing time.
