# Competitive and substitute research

Status: Desk-research baseline, not market validation
Date: 2026-08-25

## What the market already proves

The ingredients are individually established. AI journals provide entry feedback and periodic pattern summaries; canvases support spatial thought; scheduled agents react to triggers; and innovation platforms enrich and cluster submissions. Novelty therefore cannot rest on asynchronous processing alone.

## Adjacent-category lessons

### AI journals

Rosebud’s weekly report already analyzes themes and patterns across a week. Day One already offers AI-assisted writing and reflection inside a trusted journal. Slow Garden cannot win by making a prettier weekly summary. It must demonstrate that revisioned spatial context, earned delay, bounded density, and correction produce a qualitatively different result.

### Visual canvases and knowledge gardens

Obsidian Canvas, Milanote, and Heptabase already make notes spatial and connected. Obsidian’s open [JSON Canvas](https://obsidian.md/ru/blog/json-canvas/) also sets an important portability expectation. Slow Garden must make the temporal pass and source-linked return indispensable; animation alone is not differentiation.

### Scheduled agents

Notion Custom Agents already run on schedules and events and can act on a workspace. Slow Garden intentionally offers less authority: its distinctive behavior is waiting, interpreting cautiously, and returning for review. Any later Codex workbench should preserve this proposal boundary rather than compete on autonomy.

### Innovation management

HYPE and Wazoku already position AI around idea enrichment, clustering, and innovation workflows. The later “innovation greenhouse” opportunity is credible but not empty. Its advantage would need to come from sustained author incubation, transparent enrichment, and a calm review experience—not generic research expansion.

## Differentiation claim to test

Slow Garden combines four interactions that adjacent products usually separate:

1. uninterrupted source capture;
2. spatial and chronological accumulation;
3. intentionally delayed, cross-source processing;
4. a scarce, visual, source-linked return with correction and no automatic action.

The research burden is to show that the combination changes thinking quality, not merely product atmosphere.

## Near-term comparison set

Prototype evaluation should compare the same corpus and question across:

- a normal journal with no model response;
- immediate per-entry reflection;
- a conventional weekly theme summary;
- a manual spatial canvas;
- Slow Garden’s delayed three-bloom return.

Score useful surprise, faithfulness, accumulation dependence, emotional overreach, cognitive load, correction quality, and desire to continue thinking.
