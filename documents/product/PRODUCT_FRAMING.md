# Product framing and differentiated wedge

Status: H0 working baseline
Date: 2026-08-25
Audience: Individuals who want private, interruption-free reflection

## The problem

Fast conversational AI makes a useful assumption for many tasks: the person wants an answer now. That assumption is wrong during early thought. Immediate interpretation can redirect attention, prematurely name a pattern, reward completion over ambiguity, and turn reflection into a dialogue with the model.

Slow Garden gives unfinished thought somewhere useful to remain unfinished. The person captures and arranges material without machine interruption. A bounded pass works later across a revisioned snapshot and returns only when it has source-supported value to add.

## Jobs to be done

### Personal reflection

When observations accumulate across days or weeks, help me notice a recurring theme, changed belief, or unresolved tension without diagnosing me, narrating my life for me, or rewriting my original entries.

### Decision incubation

When I am living with a consequential choice, let me collect evidence and reactions at my own pace, then show me where my criteria conflict, what changed, and what remains unsupported before I decide.

### Creative problem solving

When an essay, product idea, or strategy is still fragmentary, preserve the fragments and their spatial relationships, then return with a few non-obvious connections or questions that help me continue the work.

### Research gardening

When sources and questions accumulate over time, distinguish what the sources say from what I infer, identify contradictions and gaps, and prepare bounded research without turning the space into a report feed.

## Current-workflow boundary map

This is a role inventory, not a replacement plan and not a review of private entry contents.

| Existing surface | What it is authoritative for | Friction Slow Garden may address | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper and GoodNotes | Free-form private thinking and handwriting | Connections across sessions are largely manual | Do not require migration; later capture may accept export or image references |
| Journals and daily notes | Chronological first-person record | Chronology can hide relationships across time | Original journal remains original evidence |
| Kyle Knowledge | Curated, accepted durable meaning | Unfinished thought should not pollute accepted knowledge | Only an explicitly accepted bloom may later be promoted, with provenance |
| Agent Ops | Transactional execution and portfolio truth | It is intentionally operational, not contemplative | Slow Garden must never report delivery state or become a second task system |
| Personal OS | Command-center access to projects, tasks, and agent activity | Its density and operational purpose leave little quiet thinking space | Slow Garden remains a focused personal product, not a dashboard module in V1 |
| ChatGPT and Codex | Immediate synthesis, research, and implementation | Conversational turn-taking can interrupt incubation | Slow Garden may prepare a workflow proposal; execution remains elsewhere and approval-gated |
| Project notes and forms | Rough product and innovation inputs | Enrichment is periodic and separated from authoring | Later workbench experiments may reuse the temporal loop without adding automation to V1 |

## The wedge

The smallest differentiated product is not “an AI journal,” “a canvas with AI,” or “a scheduled agent.” It is:

> A quiet spatial surface for unfinished thought where accumulation and delay are first-class inputs, and the return is a small, designed, source-linked event that the person can correct or decline.

Six properties must coexist:

1. Capture is useful before AI and remains silent while the person is thinking.
2. The model works across a frozen, revisioned collection rather than responding to one entry.
3. Delay and evidence accumulation are visible parts of the interaction model.
4. A return contains at most three earned blooms, including the valid possibility of no bloom.
5. Every interpretation separates source, inference, research, uncertainty, and contradiction.
6. The system may prepare but does not execute consequential work.

Removing any two of these collapses the concept into a well-served adjacent category.

## Category and language candidates

| Candidate | Strength | Risk | Test question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temporal co-thinking | Names time as an input and preserves partnership | Abstract and technical | Can a first-time user explain it after one screen? |
| Thinking garden | Concrete, spatial, and consistent with the experience | May sound decorative or lightweight | Does it signal serious decision and research work? |
| Slow AI | Memorable contrast with current products | Can imply slow performance rather than intentional cadence | Do users expect latency or reflection? |
| Asynchronous prompting | Accurately names notes that trigger later work | Sounds infrastructure-led and agentic | Does it obscure the quiet human foreground? |

“Slow Garden” remains the working name. A final name should be calm when spoken, work beyond journaling, avoid therapy and sentience claims, be searchable, and allow seeds, passes, and blooms without forcing every feature into a botanical metaphor.

## Product principles

- The foreground belongs to the person; the background belongs to bounded processing.
- Silence is a feature. No response is a valid and sometimes superior outcome.
- Originals are evidence. Derived material never silently replaces them.
- The user owns meaning. The system offers interpretations with reasons and uncertainty.
- Returns are scarce and composed, not feeds optimized for engagement.
- Spatiality supports thought; it does not become an infinite-canvas tax.
- Beauty should reduce pressure and clarify state, never disguise unsupported claims.
- Preparation and execution are separate permission domains.

## Non-goals

- A chatbot with a garden skin.
- A mood tracker, therapist, diagnostic system, or emotional score.
- A general personal knowledge base, project dashboard, or task manager.
- Autonomous background mutation of files, code, messages, or external systems.
- A daily streak, notification habit loop, or volume-based engagement product.
- A comprehensive life story generated from private material.

## Disconfirming evidence

Stop or reposition the concept if controlled studies show any of the following:

- Delayed cross-entry synthesis does not outperform a conventional weekly summary on useful surprise and actionability while maintaining faithfulness.
- People primarily value the visuals or ritual, but cannot name a connection enabled by accumulation.
- Spatial capture creates enough friction that people move composition elsewhere and only import finished text.
- Source and inference distinctions remain confusing in realistic use.
- Corrections do not restore trust after a wrong or emotionally overreaching interpretation.
- Sparse periods encourage fabricated meaning or pressure to write for the model.
- A quiet journal plus an explicit periodic Codex prompt delivers equivalent value with materially less complexity.

## H0 evidence still required

- Complete the two-week diary study with concrete positive and negative-control moments.
- Test category language through direct use and with 5 to 8 reflective knowledge workers.
- Run the delayed-versus-immediate comparative study rather than treating this framing as validation.
