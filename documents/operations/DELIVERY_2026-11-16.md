## Review record

Each database change had an independent adversarial review before merge, and the
repository's automated reviewers (Codex, Vercel) ran on every PR. Findings that were
confirmed with a failing test and fixed before merge:

- Deletion: account erasure with a stale session; deleted text surviving a
  concurrent pass; direct deletes bypassing scrubbing; dispatched passes that carried
  deleted writing in corrections or the index; labels from withdrawn passes left in
  later indexes.
- Activation: model text in worker error responses; worker secret readable through
  the scheduling extension's queue (redesigned as single-use tick tokens); a pass
  stranded by a race with allowlist removal; the tick not inert while disabled;
  charges booked when nothing was sent; a NULL-unsafe lease check; the dispatch limit
  miscounted; a tautological `store:false` test; a receipt that passed unmeasured
  thresholds; the nightly soft cap checked once per run.
- Data layer: an explicit server store silently answered by the browser store.
