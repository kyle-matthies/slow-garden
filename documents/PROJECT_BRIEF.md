# Project brief

Status: Accepted concept direction
Audience: Kyle-first private lab
Initial platforms: Responsive web, followed by native iOS if the core loop validates
Working category: Temporal co-thinking / contemplative AI

## Thesis

Most AI interfaces collapse human thought and machine processing into one interruptive loop: prompt, wait, react. Slow Garden separates them.

The foreground belongs to the person. It is quiet, spatial, editable, and allowed to remain unfinished. The background belongs to the system. It periodically works across accumulated material and returns later with a small number of meaningful, source-linked interpretations.

The unit of interaction is:

`seed -> incubation -> garden pass -> bloom -> human response`

The AI operates on relationships across time, not merely on the text within one entry.

## Primary outcome

Help a person discover recurring themes, changing beliefs, unresolved tensions, and surprising connections without replacing their voice or forcing immediate dialogue.

The first product must be useful as a thinking environment before any AI result arrives.

## Initial experience

1. Kyle creates or edits seeds: notes, fragments, questions, observations, links, or images.
2. Seeds can be arranged spatially into beds and loose clusters without requiring a taxonomy.
3. Capture remains silent. The system does not interrupt, complete sentences, critique, or rearrange the garden.
4. A scheduled or manual garden pass reads a revisioned snapshot.
5. On return, the garden reveals no more than three blooms: a connection, tension, shift, or question.
6. Each bloom distinguishes source evidence, system inference, and external research.
7. Kyle may keep, correct, prune, annotate, or promote a bloom into a new user-owned seed.

The primary experience has two views of the same garden. Meadow is the open capture and exploration surface; Cabinet is the focused review surface where a selected living flower becomes a pressed specimen beside its exact source clippings. Switching views changes disclosure, not data or authority.

## Processing modes

- **Tend:** organize, label, deduplicate, and identify unfinished material without adding interpretive claims.
- **Connect:** identify recurring themes, tensions, changes, and questions. This is the default.
- **Explore:** prepare source-linked external research and possible extensions. This is opt-in per garden.

Cadence and processing depth are separate controls. Weekly is the provisional default for deep passes; manual passes support deliberate incubation.

## V1 boundaries

In scope:

- Private, single-user capture and spatial organization.
- Revision history and source-linked derived artifacts.
- Asynchronous processing with visible status and failure recovery.
- A designed return and review ritual.
- User correction and learning signals.
- Responsive web use on desktop and iPhone.

Out of scope:

- Team collaboration or shared gardens.
- Mental-health diagnosis, treatment, or clinical claims.
- Automatic publication, messaging, code changes, or external-system mutation.
- A general automation builder.
- A chatbot as the primary interface.
- Native iOS before the web loop is validated.
- Importing the whole personal knowledge system before bounded-source retrieval is proven.

## Expansion paths

- Decision garden for complex personal or professional choices.
- Creative problem garden for essays, product ideas, and strategy.
- Research garden for accumulated sources and evolving questions.
- Longitudinal personal reflection across life themes.
- Codex workbench where project notes become reviewable workflow proposals.
- Innovation greenhouse where team submissions mature into researched concepts.

These are separate horizons. They must reuse a validated temporal interaction model rather than dilute V1.

## Success definition

The concept is validated when a four-week private study shows all of the following:

- Capture remains useful and interruption-free without waiting for AI.
- Delayed processing identifies relationships that were not available or obvious at entry time.
- At least one returned bloom per active week is kept, extended, or discussed.
- Every retained claim can be traced to source material and corrected.
- Empty or sparse periods do not produce manufactured meaning.
- The return feels bounded rather than overwhelming.
- Original material survives edits, reruns, failures, and rejected interpretations unchanged.
